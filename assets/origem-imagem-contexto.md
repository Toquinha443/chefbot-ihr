## Prompt utilizado

Create one editorial illustration for a Brazilian university human-robot interaction presentation, landscape 3:2. This scene illustrates a HYPOTHETICAL everyday kitchen BEFORE any robot is introduced. Absolutely no robots, no futuristic devices, no product branding, no text or typography. Show a 28-year-old adult woman with medium brown shoulder-length hair, simple dark teal casual shirt, glancing between a recipe on a smartphone propped on the counter and ingredients, with a gently thoughtful expression. A 52-year-old adult man, short salt-and-pepper hair, simple light shirt, is nearby offering a bowl while leaving her workspace clear. Natural warm kitchen light, modest contemporary shared home kitchen, wooden cutting board, tomatoes, leafy greens, dishes and spoon. Convey divided attention and cooperation, not panic or danger. Both adults fully visible from waist up, hands anatomically natural. Restrained polished editorial photography style with soft blue and warm neutral colors, ample space around people for cropping into slide image areas. This is a new fictional illustration, not actual research participants.
